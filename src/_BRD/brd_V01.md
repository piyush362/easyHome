Parent-Friendly Android Launcher
Document Type: Business Requirements Document (BRD)
Product Stage: Product Definition / MVP Planning
Version: 1.0

1. Executive Summary
   EasyHome is a simple, parent-friendly Android launcher designed to make smartphones easier and safer for elderly parents and family members who are not comfortable with modern smartphone interfaces.
   Modern smartphones provide hundreds of features, settings, applications, notifications, and gestures. While these features are useful for experienced users, they can make smartphones confusing for elderly users.
   EasyHome simplifies the phone experience by putting the things a parent uses most directly in front of them:
   Calling loved ones
   WhatsApp
   Camera
   Selfie
   Video recording
   YouTube
   Instagram
   Photos
   Medicine reminders
   Flashlight
   Weather
   Emergency help
   The product is designed around one central principle:
   A parent should be able to use their phone without constantly needing to ask their children for help.
   EasyHome also introduces a Family Setup experience, where children or caregivers can configure the parent's phone, customize the interface, select important contacts and apps, configure reminders, and protect important settings.

2. Problem Statement
   Smartphones have become increasingly powerful, but they have also become increasingly complicated.
   For many elderly users, common tasks can involve unnecessary steps:
   Calling a family member
   Normal smartphone:
   Open Phone → Contacts → Search → Find Contact → Tap Contact → Call
   EasyHome:
   Tap Daughter → Call
   Taking a selfie
   Normal smartphone:
   Open Camera → Change camera → Find selfie mode → Take photo
   EasyHome:
   Tap SELFIE
   Finding WhatsApp
   Normal smartphone:
   Find WhatsApp among many applications → Open WhatsApp → Find the person
   EasyHome:
   Tap WHATSAPP
   Remembering medicine
   Normal smartphone:
   The user may forget the time or dismiss a generic alarm without understanding what it means.
   EasyHome:
   💊 Medicine Reminder
   It is time to take your medicine.
   The opportunity is not to create another smartphone.
   The opportunity is to remove unnecessary complexity from the smartphone that already exists.

3. Product Vision
   Vision
   To create the simplest and most comfortable smartphone experience for parents and elderly users.
   Product Promise
   Everything important is right in front of you.
   EasyHome should make a parent feel:
   Comfortable
   Independent
   Safe
   Connected to family
   Confident using their phone
   The product should not feel like a "senior citizen" or "medical" device.
   It should feel like a beautiful, modern smartphone customized specifically for them.

4. Target Users
   Primary User — Parent / Elderly User
   Typical characteristics:
   Uses a smartphone for basic daily activities
   May not be comfortable navigating complex interfaces
   Frequently calls family members
   Uses WhatsApp
   Watches YouTube
   Takes photos and selfies
   May use Instagram
   Needs reminders
   May have difficulty reading small text
   May accidentally change settings
   May need assistance from children to configure the phone
   The primary user should require minimal technical knowledge.

Secondary User — Child / Family Member
This is an important part of the product.
The child may:
Set up the parent's phone
Add family contacts
Select important apps
Choose colors and themes
Increase text/icon sizes
Configure medicine reminders
Configure emergency contacts
Protect important settings
Help maintain the parent's phone experience
Therefore, EasyHome has two audiences:
Parent = daily user
Child = setup and support user

5. Core Product Principles
   Every product decision should follow these principles.
   5.1 Simplicity First
   If something can be done in one tap, it should not require three taps.
   5.2 Large and Clear
   Buttons, text, icons, and important information should be easy to see and tap.
   5.3 Familiar
   Use familiar concepts rather than teaching users new interaction patterns.
   5.4 Family First
   The people the parent loves should be more accessible than applications and settings.
   5.5 Calm
   The interface should not feel busy, stressful, or overwhelming.
   5.6 Safe
   Important actions should be protected from accidental changes.
   5.7 Personal
   The phone should feel like it belongs to the parent.

6. Core User Experience
   When the parent unlocks the phone, they should immediately see the things that matter most.
   The home screen should prioritize:
   Time
   Family
   Communication
   Camera
   Entertainment
   Reminders
   Safety
   The parent should not need to understand the concept of an "app drawer" or navigate complicated menus for common activities.

7. Home Screen Requirements
   The home screen is the most important part of EasyHome.
   It should provide immediate access to the parent's most frequently used functions.
   7.1 Clock
   Display:
   Large current time
   Day
   Date
   The clock should be highly readable.
   Example:
   10:42 AM
   Thursday, August 27

7.2 Weather
Display simple weather information.
Example:
☀️ 29°C
Sunny
The weather should remain secondary to family and communication.

8. Family / Favorite Contacts
   Family contacts are one of the core features.
   The parent should see important people directly on the home screen.
   Example:
   Daughter
   Son
   Wife
   Brother
   Grandson
   Each contact should have:
   Photo
   Name
   Relationship
   The user should be able to quickly access communication options.
   Possible actions:
   Call
   WhatsApp
   Video call
   Message
   The exact available actions can depend on the parent's installed applications.

9. WhatsApp Access
   WhatsApp should have a dedicated, highly visible entry point.
   The objective is not to recreate WhatsApp.
   The objective is to make WhatsApp immediately accessible.
   Potential future functionality may include:
   Favorite WhatsApp contacts
   WhatsApp call shortcuts
   WhatsApp video call shortcuts
   Family WhatsApp shortcuts
   The parent should not need to search through the phone to find WhatsApp.

10. Camera Experience
    Camera access should be simplified.
    Instead of one generic camera button, EasyHome should provide separate actions:
    📸 PHOTO
    Immediately opens the photo-taking experience.
    🤳 SELFIE
    Immediately opens the selfie experience.
    🎥 VIDEO
    Immediately opens video recording.
    This removes the need for the parent to understand camera modes.
    Camera functionality should be simple and focused.

11. Entertainment
    EasyHome should provide quick access to entertainment applications that parents commonly use.
    Initial focus:
    YouTube
    One large, clearly labeled button.
    Instagram
    One large, clearly labeled button.
    The purpose is simply:
    Open the app quickly.
    Future versions may introduce family-configured shortcuts or favorite content.

12. Medicine & Reminder System
    Reminders are an important part of the product.
    The home screen should clearly communicate upcoming reminders.
    Example:
    💊 Medicine
    Next reminder: 1:00 PM
    When a reminder is due, the user should receive a clear local notification.
    Example:
    Medicine Reminder
    It is time for your scheduled medicine.
    The reminder system should support general reminders as well, such as:
    Medicine
    Doctor appointments
    Water
    Exercise
    Important events
    Family occasions
    EasyHome should remain a reminder product, not a medical diagnosis or treatment application.

13. Flashlight
    Provide a large, obvious flashlight/torch button.
    The experience should be extremely simple:
    🔦 TORCH
    Tap to turn on.
    Tap again to turn off.

14. Photos
    Photos should be easily accessible.
    The parent should be able to quickly open their photos without navigating through the standard Android app interface.
    Future versions may include:
    Family Photos
    A dedicated place for family-shared memories.
    Example:
    ❤️ Family Photos
    “New photos from your daughter”
    This can become a strong emotional component of the product in future versions.

15. Emergency / Help
    EasyHome should provide an easily accessible help function.
    Example:
    🆘 HELP
    Depending on configuration, the parent may be able to:
    Call a selected family member
    Contact an emergency number
    Share their location
    Important emergency actions should have safeguards against accidental activation.
    The experience should feel calm and trustworthy, not frightening.

16. Battery Awareness
    Battery status should be visible without requiring the parent to open settings.
    Example:
    🔋 78%
    When the battery becomes low, EasyHome can provide a simple reminder:
    Battery is low
    Please connect your charger.
    The goal is to make important phone information understandable without technical terminology.

17. Location Assistance
    A future feature can allow the parent to easily access their current location.
    Example:
    📍 MY LOCATION
    Possible actions:
    View current location
    Share location with family
    This could be particularly useful while traveling or when the parent needs to tell someone where they are.

18. Family Setup
    Family Setup is one of the most important differentiating features.
    The parent should not be expected to configure complicated settings.
    Instead, the child can set up the phone.
    The child should be able to configure:
    People
    Add family members
    Set profile photos
    Choose relationships
    Choose preferred communication methods
    Apps
    Choose which applications appear on the parent's home screen.
    Appearance
    Theme
    Accent color
    Text size
    Icon size
    Button size
    Light/dark appearance
    Reminders
    Add medicine reminders
    Add appointments
    Add general reminders
    Safety
    Emergency contact
    Help settings
    Location sharing preferences

19. Personalization
    The phone should feel personal rather than generic.
    Children should be able to customize the parent's experience.
    Examples:
    Color Themes
    Ocean
    Green
    Rose
    Warm
    Blue
    Dark
    Text Size
    Large
    Extra Large
    Icon Size
    Large
    Extra Large
    Home Screen
    Choose which actions appear.
    This allows each parent's phone to be configured according to their individual needs.

20. Protected Settings
    A major usability problem is accidental configuration changes.
    EasyHome should allow important settings to be protected.
    For example:
    The parent can use the phone normally, but changing:
    Home screen layout
    Family contacts
    Theme
    Emergency settings
    Important applications
    Family configuration
    may require a family PIN or protected access.
    This gives children confidence that the launcher will remain configured correctly.

21. Simple App Access
    Not every application needs to appear on the home screen.
    EasyHome should separate:
    Important Apps
    Apps the parent uses frequently.
    All Apps
    A simple place where less frequently used apps can be found.
    The parent should never be overwhelmed by a screen containing dozens of icons.

22. Notifications
    Notifications should be simplified wherever possible.
    Instead of forcing the parent to understand numerous notification icons, EasyHome can eventually present important notifications in a more understandable way.
    Example:
    💬 New message from Daughter
    📞 You missed a call from Son
    This feature should be considered carefully because notifications can become overwhelming if not filtered properly.

23. Voice Assistance — Future Feature
    A future version could allow the parent to perform simple actions using voice.
    Example:
    “Call my daughter.”
    “Open WhatsApp.”
    “Open camera.”
    “What time is it?”
    This should be considered a future enhancement rather than an MVP requirement.

26. Non-Goals
    EasyHome should not attempt to become:
    A complete Android replacement
    A medical application
    A social network
    A messaging platform
    A replacement for WhatsApp
    A replacement for YouTube
    A full parental-control application
    A complicated productivity application
    The product's job is to simplify access to existing smartphone functionality.

29. Product Differentiation
    EasyHome should not compete by having more features than other launchers.
    It should compete by being more thoughtful.
    Traditional Launcher
    Apps first.
    EasyHome
    People first.
    Traditional launchers ask:
    “Which app do you want?”
    EasyHome asks:
    “What do you want to do?”
    Examples:
    Instead of:
    Camera
    EasyHome offers:
    📸 Photo
    🤳 Selfie
    🎥 Video
    Instead of:
    Contacts
    EasyHome offers:
    ❤️ Daughter
    ❤️ Son
    ❤️ Wife
    This distinction should be central to the product.

    The initial product should prioritize trust and adoption.
    A possible model is:
    Free
    Basic launcher
    Family contacts
    Essential apps
    Basic customization
    Basic reminders
    Premium
    Potential premium features:
    Advanced personalization
    Multiple parent profiles
    Backup
    Advanced family configuration
    Remote family management
    Family photo features
    Advanced reminder functionality
    Advertising should be avoided in sensitive or safety-related experiences such as emergency/help and medicine reminders.

35. Final Product Definition
    EasyHome is not simply an Android launcher with bigger icons.
    It is a family-configured smartphone experience for elderly parents.
    The product solves a simple but meaningful problem:
    Parents want to use their phones independently, while children want their parents to have a phone that is simple, safe, and easy to understand.
    EasyHome connects those two needs.
    The parent gets:
    Simple access
    Large controls
    Family at their fingertips
    Easy WhatsApp access
    Easy camera access
    Entertainment
    Reminders
    Safety
    The child gets:
    Control over setup
    Personalization
    Protection from accidental changes
    A simpler way to help their parent
    Confidence that important features are easy to reach
    The ultimate goal is not to make the phone look different.
    The goal is to make the phone feel effortless.
    EasyHome
    A simpler phone for the people you love.
